## Scope

Scientific visualization sits inside the plasma-source effort and extends outward to every subteam:

- Extend particle visualization to **engineering geometry and field data**
- Produce **scientific figures** for papers and technical reviews
- Build this **club homepage**, with interactive views of the underlying data

## Where it started

The spring 2026 prototype rendered **505 tracked particles across 150 frames**: neutral argon, metastable argon, argon ions, and electrons. It was built on synthetic data generated by a Python stepwise-ionization solver so the visualization tooling could mature before real simulation output arrived.

## This semester

- **Club homepage**: this site. Next up are interactive viewers, driven by simulation data that is precomputed in Python and rendered in the browser.
- **CAD and figures**: a simplified model of the hollow cathode assembly for cutaway renders.
- **Research paper**: a team paper on **ion kinetics (stepwise ionization) simulation**, targeting the spring 2027 Engineering Open House and Undergraduate Research Symposium.
- **Reproducible figures**: every published figure records its source data, units, and model assumptions.
